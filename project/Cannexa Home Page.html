02 · The pathway

A four-step clinical pathway. No shortcuts.

01

Eligibility screen

Complete a structured medical questionnaire. Our clinical team reviews your history, current medications, and past treatments against NICE guidance.

~ 10 minutes · free
02

Specialist consultation

A 30-minute video appointment with a GMC-registered consultant. You discuss symptoms, goals, risks, and — if appropriate — a treatment plan.

30 minutes · £95
03

Prescription & dispense

If prescribed, your medicine is dispensed by a GPhC-registered pharmacy from an EU-GMP source, with a batch-specific Certificate of Analysis.

2–4 working days
04

Structured follow-up

Titration is reviewed at week two, month one, and quarterly thereafter. Dosage is adjusted to outcome — not to an assumed schedule.

Ongoing · included
