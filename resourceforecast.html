Clinical resource & cost forecasting

Turn a demand forecast into a costed hiring plan

Demand is split across clinician groups by each service's order share, converted to hours using prescribing time per order, and compared to your team (including already-planned hires). Calls & messages from those orders drive patient-support headcount. Each resource type gets a single total-headcount-needed line by month; gaps become recommended hires with lead-time-aware start dates and phased cost. Everything is computed in your browser — no data ever leaves this machine.

2 · Config

Every parameter used in the run — fully editable. Add groups, roles, services or attributes as data, not code.
Editable model
Global
currency, forecast window
Clinician groups
demand pools that services route work to — each has a colour used throughout
Roles
each belongs to a group · cost/hour, productivity %, lead times

Productive hours/FTE = contracted × productivity %. Monthly cost = cost/hour × contracted hours. A group's gap is filled by its cheapest-capable role.

Services
category, and a list of tasks (role + time/order + how often)

Each task is one role doing one activity. Time is seconds per order; incidence is the % of orders that need it (100% = every order; use it for prescriber/GP split, or a prescreen→consult funnel). Task hours = orders × incidence% × time ÷ 3600. An order can span many tasks across different roles (async review + messaging, or sync prescreen + consult + GP letter).

Contact model · support-role headcount from calls & messages

Total orders × calls/order & messages/order = contact volume. Each support role takes a % share of calls and of messages, handles that many per day, and delivers its productivity %. Required FTE = (call-days + message-days) ÷ (working days × productivity).

4 · People mapping

Roster people (filtered to your clinical Cost Centre) → clinician role, hours & specialisms, by staff number. New/changed people are flagged to map before a run; mappings persist in your config.
—
⚠️
Map the flagged people first. New staff numbers (no rule matched) are highlighted and block export until each is given a role (or set to Ignore).
Mapping rules — Job code + Location Name → role (first match wins; edit to match your policy)

Hours default to full-time until you set them; specialisms use the same syntax as the HR file (Skin; Sexual Health; -Psoriasis). Staff numbers are stored in the config; names stay in the uploaded file only.
