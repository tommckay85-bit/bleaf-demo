How it works

A consultation built around you, not a checkout

Medicinal cannabis is a prescription-only medicine. Our pathway is designed so a specialist gets the full picture before anything is prescribed — and stays with you afterwards.

Answer a short, confidential set of questions about your symptoms, history and current medicines. A clinician reviews it — there's no charge to find out if you're suitable.

~10 minutes · free

If prescribed, your medicine is dispensed by a GPhC-registered pharmacy and delivered discreetly to your door, with batch testing behind every order.

A few working days
